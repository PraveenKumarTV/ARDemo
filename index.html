🕌 Taj Mahal

Location: Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India

The Taj Mahal is a white-marble mausoleum commissioned by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal.

The principal mausoleum was substantially completed in the late 1630s, while work on the wider complex continued afterward.

Architecture: The complex combines a central mausoleum, four minarets, a large central dome, subsidiary domes, gateways, mosque/guest-house structures and formal gardens.

This EX7 model is a simplified procedural 3D reconstruction created directly in JavaScript using Three.js.
