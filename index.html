🛕 Meenakshi Amman Temple

Location: Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India

The Meenakshi Amman Temple is a historic Hindu temple complex in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, dedicated to Goddess Meenakshi and Lord Sundareswarar.

The temple complex developed over many centuries, with major expansions and construction taking place under successive Pandya and Nayak rulers.

Architecture: The complex is known for its towering gopurams, sculpted figures, mandapams, corridors and colorful Dravidian temple architecture.

This EX7 model is a simplified procedural 3D reconstruction of the temple created directly in JavaScript using Three.js.
