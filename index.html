🏛️ Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE)

Location: Thiruparankundram, Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India

Thiagarajar College of Engineering was founded in 1957 by philanthropist Karumuttu Thiagarajan Chettiar and is one of the well-known engineering institutions of southern Tamil Nadu.

Campus: The main academic block is the campus landmark, with a central entrance portico, a tower above it and classroom wings on both sides.

This EX7 model is a simplified procedural 3D representation of the TCE main building created directly in JavaScript using Three.js. Proportions and colors are approximate.
